FDM Dockside Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Freeport Diesel and Marine, LLC operates FDM Dockside, a work management app for authorized employees connected to FDM Manager.

Information we handle

We handle employee names, employee identifiers, authentication information, work schedules, clock-in and clock-out records, job time entries, job notes, parts usage, and photos employees choose to upload. The app also displays customer and job information maintained by the business.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate employees, assign and manage work, record time, document jobs, track parts, and support business operations. Authorized office staff can access work records through FDM Manager.

Camera access

The app requests camera permission to scan parts barcodes and take job photos. Uploading job photos is optional. FDM Dockside does not use GPS to track employee location.

Storage and service providers

Work records and uploaded photos are stored on our backend. We use hosting and infrastructure providers, including Railway, to operate the service and process information on our behalf. Data sent to our production backend is transferred over HTTPS.

Disclosure of information

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Information may be disclosed to service providers supporting our operations, when required by law, or when you direct us to disclose it. Job documentation may be provided to the relevant customer as part of our services.

Retention and deletion

We retain information as needed for employment, payroll, service records, business operations, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your information. Some records may need to be retained. Disabling an employee account does not automatically delete historical work records.

Accounts

Employee accounts are created and managed by the business. The app does not offer public account registration.

Children

FDM Dockside is intended for authorized adult workers and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app or our practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
